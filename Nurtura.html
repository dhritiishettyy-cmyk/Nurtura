A few things worth knowing

Why does maternal care include emotional wellbeing?

Pregnancy and the period after childbirth can bring emotional as well as physical changes. Your feelings deserve attention and support, too.

When should I seek professional advice?

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional if you have symptoms that worry you, questions about recovery, or emotional distress that feels difficult to manage. Seek urgent care for emergencies.

Can Nurtura replace a doctor?

No. Nurtura is intended for general education and support. It cannot diagnose, prescribe treatment or replace personalised medical care.
